# Privacy Policy **Last updated: September 27, 2026** This Privacy Policy describes how the TrueNAS WoL Alexa Skill handles information. ## Information We Use The TrueNAS WoL Alexa Skill is a personal home automation service used to wake a device on a private home network using Wake-on-LAN. The service may process the following information: * Amazon account information required by Amazon Login with Amazon (LWA) for account linking. * An access token provided by Amazon for authentication. * The MAC address of the Wake-on-LAN device configured by the skill owner. ## How Information Is Used The information is used only to: * authenticate the Alexa user; * identify the configured Wake-on-LAN device; * allow Alexa to control the configured device; * send a Wake-on-LAN request through a compatible Echo device on the local network. The service does not use this information for advertising, profiling, or marketing. ## Information Sharing The service does not sell or share personal information with third parties. Amazon services, including Alexa and Login with Amazon, may process information according to Amazon's own privacy policies and terms. ## Data Storage The skill does not intentionally collect or maintain a database of users or personal information. Authentication information is handled through Amazon Login with Amazon and the Alexa service. ## Data Retention The skill does not intentionally retain personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the account-linking and Wake-on-LAN functionality. ## Contact This is a personal, non-commercial home automation project. For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the skill owner through the GitHub repository hosting this page. ## Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of the skill changes. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
